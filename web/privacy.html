ComboFox Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

Data We Collect

ComboFox may collect your email address, display name, profile photo URL, and user-generated app content (favorites, collection items, and gameplay-related entries).

How We Use Data

We use your data to create and maintain your account, sync your app content between devices, and provide support responses when you contact us.

Services We Use

ComboFox uses Firebase services, including Authentication, Cloud Firestore, and Cloud Storage, to store and process data.

Data Deletion

You can request account deletion directly in the app from your profile. Deletion requests remove your account profile and associated app data we control.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact support@combofox.net.
